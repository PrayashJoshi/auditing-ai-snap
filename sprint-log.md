- 2026-11-13: METHODS AUDIT of the design. Facts found: (a) Chohlas-Wood Table B1 treats cause codes 1, 3, 4 and 7 as undetectable and only code 2 of our client-side set {1,2,3,4,7} as detectable, so his 80/80 is not like-for-like on most of the human arm; (b) his category check succeeds with 0.5 (10 min) and his flag check 0.8 (5 min), his rule has no 80/80 parameter, and he reports no calibration step or confidence intervals; (c) FY2024 QC has no stratified sample (STRATUM=0, state is the only stratum), 500 official replicate weights exist in a separate zip, HWGT runs 52 to 78,000; (d) the 2,096 rebuildable filter never applied AMTERR>56, and 299 of them are in the 12 multi-row SUA jurisdictions; (e) ACTNTYPE 1 and 2 separates certification from recertification. Flaws found: document availability and AI error prevalence are both set by our own withholding rule; a deterministic missing-document or value-mismatch checker is an unlisted rival reader; category-check labels are 50% sensitive, so learned miss rates are biased low; greedy selection gives inclusion probability 0 or 1, so Horvitz-Thompson only works on a random slice; pilot households used for prompt tuning must leave the evaluation set. Also accepted from the design review: a flag on an undocumented fact group should not get the 5-minute/0.8 flag-check price; with the agent seeing only the form and packet, it cannot guess a fact it does not know exists; report dollars remaining beside capture; normalise dollars-per-review by the share of error dollars caught, since it confounds error prevalence with rule quality. Verified in the local Chohlas-Wood text: Appendix D.8 (flag categories ranked by historical correctable dollars per expected hour) and D.9 (combined rule in risk order throughout) exist; a "27 of 27 jurisdictions" claim for D.9 was not found and is not cited.
